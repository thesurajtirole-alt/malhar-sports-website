## Sawaal: School Sports Day Ke Liye Kya Kya Chahiye?

Sports day ek hi din hota hai, aur last-minute scramble se bachne ke liye ye checklist kaam aayegi.

## Quick Answer

Core essentials: **sahi PT shoes**, **house-color kit**, **water bottle**, aur agar specific event mein participate kar rahe ho (running, long jump, relay), uske hisaab se additional gear.

## Detailed Guide

### PT Shoes — Non-Negotiable

School ki zyadatar events (races, relay, jumps) grip aur support maangti hai. Regular casual shoes mein slip hone ka risk zyada hota hai, especially grass ya dusty ground pe. Proper PT/sports shoes already break-in ho, naye din pehle mat pehno.

### House Color Kit

Zyadatar schools house-wise T-shirts assign karte hai. Pehle se confirm kar lo ki kaunsa color chahiye, aur ek spare rakho in case wo din mil na paaye.

### Event-Specific Gear

- **Running events** — lightweight PT shoes, comfortable socks (no new socks that day — blister risk).
- **Long jump/high jump** — grip wali shoes, flexible sole.
- **Relay** — koi special gear nahi, lekin shoes secure fit ki honi chahiye taaki baton pass karte waqt slip na ho.

### Comfort Essentials

- Water bottle (label pe naam likhna mat bhoolna)
- Sunscreen agar outdoor event hai
- Extra pair of socks
- Small towel

## Common Mistakes

1. **Naye shoes ya socks sports day pe pehli baar pehen na** — blister risk high hota hai.
2. **House color kit last moment pe dhundna** — 1-2 din pehle confirm kar lo.
3. **Hydration ignore karna** — especially garmi mein, event ke beech paani zaroor piyo.
4. **Warm-up skip karna** — direct race mein cold muscles se injury ka risk badhta hai.

## Expert Tip

Agar bachcha multiple events mein participate kar raha hai, ek comfortable, all-purpose PT shoe better hai specialized shoes se — flexibility zyada important hai variety of events ke liye.
