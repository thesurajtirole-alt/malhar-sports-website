## Sawaal: Running Beginners Kaise Shuru Kare?

Bahut log excitement mein pehle hi din 5km bhaag jaate hai aur agle din pair dukhne ki wajah se chhod dete hai. Sahi tarika slow shuru karna hai.

## Quick Answer

Beginners ko **walk-run method** se start karna chahiye — thodi der bhaago, thodi der chalo, repeat. Directly non-stop running se injury aur burnout dono ka risk badhta hai.

## Detailed Guide

### Week 1-2: Walk-Run Foundation

1 minute jogging + 2 minute walking — ye cycle 15-20 minutes tak repeat karo, hafte mein 3 baar. Isse body ko gradually adapt karne ka time milta hai.

### Week 3-4: Running Time Badhao

Jogging time ko 2 minutes tak badhao, walking 1 minute pe le aao. Total session time same rakho (20-25 minutes), lekin ab running proportion zyada hoga.

### Week 5-6: Continuous Running Try Karo

Agar walk-run comfortable lag raha hai, 10 minutes continuous running try karo bina rukein. Agar mushkil lage, walk-run pe wapas jao — koi sharam ki baat nahi.

### Rest Days Ka Importance

Roz running karna counterproductive hai beginners ke liye. Hafte mein 3-4 running days aur baaki din rest ya light activity (walking, stretching) — muscles ko recover karne ka time chahiye.

### Sahi Pace Kaise Pehchane

Agar running karte waqt baat nahi kar paa rahe (breathless ho rahe ho), tum bahut fast bhaag rahe ho. Beginners ke liye pace itni honi chahiye ki conversation possible ho — "conversational pace" isko bolte hai.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Pehle hi din zyada distance cover karna** — motivation high hoti hai, lekin body ready nahi hoti. Injury ka sabse common reason.
2. **Stretching/warm-up skip karna** — cold muscles se strain ka risk badhta hai.
3. **Roz running karna, rest days na lena** — overuse injuries (shin splints, knee pain) ka main reason.
4. **Galat shoes mein bhaagna** — running-specific shoes zaroori hai, casual sneakers nahi.

## Expert Tip

Pehle mahine mein **distance ya speed** ka target mat rakho — sirf consistency ka target rakho. Agar 3 hafte tak regularly bhaag paaye (chahe kam distance hi ho), tum already zyada logo se aage ho jo pehle hafte mein hi chhod dete hai.

## FAQs

**Q: Running se pehle kya khana chahiye?**
Halka snack (banana, toast) 30-60 minutes pehle. Bhari khana turant pehle avoid karo — cramps ho sakte hai.

**Q: Kitni jaldi 5K bhaag paunga?**
Consistent walk-run training se zyadatar log 6-8 hafton mein 5K continuous run kar paate hai. Ye har body ke liye vary karta hai — apni progress ko doosro se compare mat karo.

**Q: Knee pain ho raha hai, kya normal hai?**
Halka soreness normal hai shuru mein, lekin sharp ya persistent pain normal nahi hai. Agar pain continue kare, rest karo aur zaroorat pade to doctor se consult karo — galat shoes bhi ek common reason hote hai.
