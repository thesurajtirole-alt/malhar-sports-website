## Sawaal: Gym Shuru Karne Se Pehle Kya Kharidna Chahiye?

Naya gym membership excitement mein liya, lekin sahi gear ke bina pehla hafta hi tough ho sakta hai. Yaha ek simple checklist hai.

## Quick Answer

Bare minimum: **gym shoes**, **comfortable clothing**, **shaker bottle**, aur **gloves** (optional but helpful). Baaki sab dhire-dhire add kar sakte ho.

## Detailed Guide

### 1. Gym Shoes — Sabse Important

Running shoes gym ke liye ideal nahi hote — unme heel cushioning zyada hoti hai jo weightlifting mein stability kam karti hai. Gym/training shoes ka flat, firm sole hota hai jo squats aur deadlifts mein better ground contact deta hai.

### 2. Comfortable, Breathable Clothing

Cotton T-shirts sweat absorb karte hai lekin dry hone mein time lete hai. Dri-fit ya polyester blend fabric better rehta hai intense workouts ke liye — sweat wick karta hai, chafing kam karta hai.

### 3. Shaker Bottle

Protein shake ho ya bas paani, ek proper shaker (leak-proof, mixing ball ke saath) roz kaam aayega. Cheap plastic bottles jaldi crack ho jaate hai regular use se.

### 4. Gloves — Optional Lekin Helpful

Zyada weight lifting karte ho to gloves palms ko calluses aur blisters se bachate hai, aur grip bhi better hoti hai sweaty hands ke saath.

### 5. Resistance Bands (Home Workouts Ke Liye)

Agar kabhi gym miss ho jaaye ya travel pe ho, resistance bands se basic workout ghar pe bhi ho sakta hai. Lightweight aur affordable.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Running shoes mein weightlifting karna** — stability issues aur injury risk badhta hai.
2. **Bahut zyada gear ek saath khareedna** — pehle basics try karo, phir zarurat ke hisaab se add karo.
3. **Cheap, non-breathable clothing** — workout uncomfortable ho jaata hai aur skin irritation ho sakti hai.
4. **Shoes ka size regular shoes jaisa hi lena** — gym shoes thodi snug fit honi chahiye, running shoes se different.

## Expert Tip

Pehle hafte mein zyada invest mat karo — basic shoes aur clothing se start karo. Jab pata chal jaaye ki tera routine kya hai (weights, cardio, ya dono), tab specific gear add karo.

## FAQs

**Q: Gym shoes aur running shoes mein kya farak hai?**
Gym shoes ka sole flatter aur firmer hota hai — stability ke liye. Running shoes mein zyada cushioning hoti hai — impact absorb karne ke liye. Dono ka purpose alag hai.

**Q: Gloves zaroori hai kya?**
Nahi, optional hai. Lekin agar heavy lifting karte ho ya hands sweat karte hai zyada, gloves grip aur comfort improve karte hai.

**Q: Budget kam hai — kya priority pe khareedu?**
Sirf gym shoes aur ek comfortable dri-fit tshirt se bhi start ho sakta hai. Baaki cheezein dheere dheere add karo.
