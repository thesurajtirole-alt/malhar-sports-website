## Sawaal: Badminton Racket Kaise Choose Kare?

"Bhaiya, koi bhi achi racket de do" — ye sabse common request hai, lekin har player ke liye "achi racket" alag hoti hai.

## Quick Answer

Racket choose karte waqt teen cheezein dekho: **weight** (U-marking), **balance point** (head-heavy vs head-light), aur **grip size**. Ye teeno tumhare playing style pe depend karte hai.

## Detailed Guide

### Step 1: Weight Samjho (U-Marking)

Rackets ka weight "U" marking se dikhaya jaata hai:

- **3U (85-89g)** — heaviest, zyada power lekin swing speed kam. Strong wrist walo ke liye.
- **4U (80-84g)** — most common, balance of power aur control. Zyada players ke liye ye sahi hai.
- **5U (75-79g)** — lightest, fast swing speed. Beginners aur fast-paced doubles players ke liye achi.

### Step 2: Balance Point Decide Karo

- **Head-heavy** — zyada smash power, lekin defense mein slow. Attacking players ke liye.
- **Even balance** — power aur control ka balance. All-round players ke liye best.
- **Head-light** — fast reactions, quick net play. Defensive ya doubles players ke liye.

### Step 3: Grip Size

Grip bahut mota ho to wrist movement restrict hoti hai; bahut patla ho to control kam milta hai. Standard grip size G4 hoti hai — agar hand chhota hai, G5 try karo.

### Step 4: String Tension

- **Beginners** — 18-21 lbs (zyada forgiving, bigger sweet spot)
- **Intermediate** — 22-25 lbs
- **Advanced** — 26-30 lbs (zyada control, lekin sweet spot chhota)

Zyada tension = zyada control, kam tension = zyada power. String bhi apni skill level ke hisaab se choose karo.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Pro players ki racket copy karna** — unki racket unke liye customize hoti hai, tumhare liye nahi.
2. **Bahut heavy racket lena "powerful lagegi" soch ke** — isse wrist injury ka risk badhta hai, especially beginners mein.
3. **String tension bahut high rakhna shuru mein** — control milta hai lekin mishits zyada painful lagte hai aur strings jaldi tootne lagti hai.
4. **Grip badalte na rehna** — grip worn out ho jaaye to racket haath se slip ho sakti hai, especially sweat mein.

## Expert Tip

Store pe aake racket swing karke dekho — kaise feel hoti hai wrist mein. Do rackets same weight ki bhi different balance ki wajah se bilkul alag feel de sakti hai.

## FAQs

**Q: Beginners ke liye kaunsi racket sahi hai?**
4U weight, even balance, aur medium tension (20-22 lbs) — ye combination most beginners ke liye forgiving aur comfortable hoti hai.

**Q: Racket kitne saal chalti hai?**
Frame proper care se 3-5 saal chal sakta hai, lekin strings har 3-6 mahine mein replace karni chahiye regular play ke saath.

**Q: Carbon fiber hi lena chahiye?**
Zyadatar modern rackets carbon fiber ki hoti hai — lightweight aur durable. Budget rackets mein aluminum bhi milta hai, lekin performance thoda kam hota hai.
