## Sawaal: Sports Shoes Ki Life Kaise Badhaye?

Achi shoes mein invest karne ke baad, thodi si care se unki life significantly badh sakti hai. Yaha simple, practical tips hai.

## Quick Answer

Teen cheezein sabse zyada farak dalti hai: **regular cleaning**, **proper storage**, aur **shoes rotate karna** (ek hi pair roz na pehno).

## Detailed Guide

### Cleaning Ka Sahi Tarika

- **Upar ki dirt** — soft brush ya damp cloth se turant clean karo, sukhne se pehle.
- **Deep clean** — mild soap aur lukewarm paani use karo. Harsh detergents fabric aur glue ko damage kar sakte hai.
- **Insoles nikaal ke alag saaf karo** — sabse zyada sweat aur smell yahi collect karta hai.
- **Washing machine mein mat dalo** — zyadatar sports shoes ka structure aur cushioning damage ho sakta hai.

### Sukhaane Ka Sahi Tarika

Direct sunlight ya heater ke paas mat sukhao — glue weak ho sakta hai aur material crack ho sakta hai. Shoes ko newspaper se stuff karke, room temperature pe, shade mein sukhao.

### Storage Tips

- Shoes ko damp/humid jagah pe mat rakho — fungus aur smell ki wajah ban sakta hai.
- Shoe trees ya crumpled paper use karo shape maintain karne ke liye.
- Direct floor ke bajaye rack ya shelf pe rakho.

### Rotation — Sabse Underrated Tip

Ek hi pair roz pehen na cushioning ko jaldi compress kar deta hai kyunki usse rest/recover karne ka time nahi milta. Agar do pairs rotate karo (especially running shoes), dono ki life badh jaati hai.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Wet shoes seedha bag mein daal dena** — smell aur fungus ka main reason.
2. **Washing machine mein dhona** — cushioning aur structure damage hota hai.
3. **Bahut tight lace karna aur phir jabardasti utaarna** — heel counter (back support) damage ho sakta hai jaldi.
4. **Sirf ek pair sab activities ke liye use karna** — running shoes mein football khelna, ya vice versa, dono shoes ki life kam karta hai.

## Expert Tip

Anti-odor insoles ya activated charcoal pouches shoes mein rakhna smell control karne ka simple, cheap tarika hai — especially monsoon season mein jab shoes properly sukh nahi paati.

## FAQs

**Q: Shoes mein smell aa rahi hai, kya kare?**
Insoles nikaal ke alag saaf karo, shoes ko achi tarah sukhne do, aur baking soda ya charcoal pouch raat bhar shoe ke andar rakho.

**Q: Kitne dino baad naya pair lena chahiye?**
Running shoes 500-800km ke baad, casual/sports shoes usage ke hisaab se 6 mahine se 1 saal.

**Q: Rain mein shoes bheeg gayi, kya kare?**
Turant newspaper se stuff karke shade mein sukhao. Direct heater/sun se bachao — material weak ho sakta hai.
